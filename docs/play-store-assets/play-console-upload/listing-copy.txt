FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
============================================================
ACOMI helps paying guest (PG) houses, hostels, and messes run day-to-day operations in one place.

Residents can join their space, see occupancy and meals, track dues, submit payment proofs, and raise complaints.

Owners and operators can manage buildings, rooms, and beds, add members, run meal polls, review payment proofs, and handle complaints.

What you can do
• Create and manage a space (PG, hostel, or mess)
• Set up accommodation: buildings, floors, rooms, and beds
• Invite and manage members
• Plan meals and collect meal responses
• Track rent and meal dues
• Upload and review payment proofs (UPI, bank transfer, cash, cheque, or other)
• Raise and resolve complaints with photos
• Complete your profile, including optional ID and address proofs

ACOMI records occupancy, meals, dues, and payment proofs for your space. It does not process card payments, store a digital wallet, or transfer money.
